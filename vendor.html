How approval works · three-person rule
1Maker — you (vendor), or Axis ops on your behalf — enter the data, upload photo and documents, submit
2Axis operator (checker) — verifies each document against the form; may send back with remarks
3Axis admin (approver) — approves — only then the van / crew member can be used on a run
Three different people, every time — also for later changes. Later changes come as new task links: renewal links go out automatically 60 / 30 / 7 days before a document expires; for anything else (new gunman, van replaced, mobile change) the vendor opens Request a link — GSTIN + registered mobile + OTP — and gets a fresh task link, or asks Axis ops who can send one or enter it themselves. No password, no account.
Status by SMS / email: you will be told when each item is checked, approved or sent back (sent-back items come with a new link to fix them). Your items at Axis: awaiting checker 3 · awaiting approver 1 · sent back 1 · active 6
