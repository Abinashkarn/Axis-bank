Track0tic for Axis Bank — Cash van tracking

Phase 1 wireframes for review. One Track0tic device per van; the record is van, driver, GPS kilometres and stops. Nothing locks in phase 1; cash boxes are phase 2.

10 screens
Driver phone
Scan device QR → OTP login → Scan van plate + odometer → Crew on board → Start duty → On duty → End duty → Device offline, plus Hindi screens
13 screens
Axis portal
Login, dashboard, vans, CIT vendors, crew members (KYC, all roles), duties, duty record, branches & chests, alerts, KM reports, users & roles, vendor view, printable gate pass

Rules shown in the screens

  • Every crew member — driver, custodian 1–2, gunman 1–2 — has full KYC (name as on Aadhaar, Aadhaar, mobile, DOB, photo, DL for drivers, arms licence for gunmen) approved by a second Axis admin. Only the driver logs in by mobile OTP; he confirms the crew on board at start of duty.
  • Every run prints as an A4 gate pass: crew with masked IDs, route, QR, and hand-written opening / closing odometer boxes; ops key the gate readings back into the portal.
  • Licence expired → driver blocked from starting duty. Expiring within 60 days → warning.
  • Every van and driver belongs to exactly one CIT vendor; several vendors can run at once; a vendor sees only its own fleet.
  • KM is measured from the device GPS track per duty and reconciled against the vendor's invoice KM per van per month.
  • Branches, ATMs and chests are geofences: arrival, departure and dwell are logged automatically; halts outside them are flagged.
  • Maps: Leaflet with MapMyIndia tiles on every screen that has a map; geocoding via MapMyIndia; no OpenStreetMap tiles for a bank tenant.
  • Driver pages in English and Hindi (full translation, toggle in the app bar).
  • Portal logins only from approved Axis networks; adding users, vendors, vans or locations needs a second Axis approver; audit log kept 5 years.
