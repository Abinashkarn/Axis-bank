Rules the system enforces
Every role — driver, custodian, gunman — needs Verified KYC before being put on a run. Any crew member on the run can log in at the van (mobile OTP) and operate the duty; a run still needs a verified driver with a valid licence on board.
Crew per run: typically 1 driver · 2 custodians · 2 gunmen, but the pass, verify page and crew phone show exactly who is on board (dynamic, any number). Below the tenant’s minimum is a warning (recorded), not a block; the bank can switch blocking on per tenant. Crew present is confirmed by the driver at start and printed on the gate pass.
Driving licence expired → driver blocked from starting a duty; vendor and Axis ops alerted.
Licence expiring within 60 days → warning on this list and on the driver’s phone.
One mobile number = one crew member = one vendor. Moving a crew member between vendors is an Axis action with a reason.
A driver can only start duty on a van that belongs to their own vendor.
Aadhaar and licence images are kept for the contract period, then purged; the audit trail of who viewed them is kept 5 years.

Rules
Stage 1 — Maker: the vendor through a one-time task link (no login) or Axis ops entering on the vendor’s behalf (flagged “entered by bank”). Enters data, uploads photo + documents, submits.
Stage 2 — Checker: bank operator. Opens the dossier, ticks the document checklist, may send back with remarks. Cannot be the maker.
Stage 3 — Approver: bank admin / super admin. Sees the checker’s ticks, approves or rejects with reason. Cannot be the maker or the checker.
Every edit, renewal or role change restarts at stage 1. All three names, times and IPs are on the dossier trail. Renewal task links go to the vendor automatically 60 / 30 / 7 days before expiry; any other change → vendor uses “Request a link” (GSTIN + registered mobile + OTP) or Axis ops sends one. Each link is single-purpose, single-use, 7 days.
