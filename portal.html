Rules the system enforces
Every role — driver, custodian, gunman — needs Verified KYC before being put on a run. Only a Driver logs in at the van and starts the duty.
Minimum crew per run: 1 driver · 1 custodian · 1 gunman (tenant setting). Crew present is confirmed by the driver at start and printed on the gate pass.
Driving licence expired → driver blocked from starting a duty; vendor and Axis ops alerted.
Licence expiring within 60 days → warning on this list and on the driver’s phone.
One mobile number = one crew member = one vendor. Moving a crew member between vendors is an Axis action with a reason.
A driver can only start duty on a van that belongs to their own vendor.
Aadhaar and licence images are kept for the contract period, then purged; the audit trail of who viewed them is kept 5 years.
